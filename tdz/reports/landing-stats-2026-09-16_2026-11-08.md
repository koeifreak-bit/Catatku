# IJV landing statistics: 16 Sep – 8 Nov 2026

Source: vAMSYS Operations API, raw ACARS data for 1,329 of the 1,635 PIREPs filed 8 Sep – 8 Nov 2026.
The download was stopped early on request, so 8 Oct – 8 Nov is complete (730/730) and 16 Sep – 7 Oct is
covered from 16 Sep onwards (599/905; 8–15 Sep is missing). Rejected PIREPs and claims are excluded, leaving 1,281
PIREPs, of which 1,266 have touchdown data.

## Definitions

- **Within TDZ:** touchdown between the threshold and the far end of the last ICAO touchdown-zone marking for that runway
  (922.5 m on runways ≥ 2400 m, fewer markings on shorter ones). Same rule the TDZ bot uses.
- **290–500 m:** touchdown 290–500 m from the start of the runway.
- **Violation:** touchdown past the last TDZ marking (the −200 rule). Only TDZ violations count.
- **Reduced flaps (Airbus only):** flaps at CONF 3 (not FULL) at the final touchdown.
- **Single-engine taxi-in (Airbus + Boeing):** after touchdown, the first engine is shut down ≥ 60 s before the last one.
  At the gate, most flights shut both down within 10 s of each other.
- The ATR 72 is not counted as Airbus.
